# BUZZELLO — Hex Strategy Game | Black-Yellow Board + Reversible Pieces

Bring BUZZELLO to the table with a bold black-and-yellow honeycomb board and matching reversible pieces. Its 61 recessed cells keep play organized, while chunky 1.3-inch pieces are easy to pick up and flip.

## Play and explore

[Play BUZZELLO online](https://aresfirst.org/buzzello) — share a device, invite a friend to a private match, or challenge three levels of computer opponent. Yellow moves first.

BUZZELLO pairs a 61-cell honeycomb strategy board with reversible BioBuzz
playing pieces. The **black-and-yellow board** has four sections joined by
dovetails. The **black-and-yellow pieces** show the honeycomb artwork on both sides.

The sturdy board uses **2.2 mm dividers**, a **2.8 mm floor**, and **2.4 mm
deep pockets**. Pieces are **1.3 inches / 33.02 mm across flats** and 4.8 mm
thick; 2.4 mm remains exposed above the dividers for lifting and flipping.
Pockets are 33.50 mm across flats.

### What to print

- Print PRINT_FIRST_Pocket_and_Joint_Test first and check both the piece fit
  and the mating dovetails.
- Print each of the four board files once: 01 top left, 02 top right,
  03 bottom left, and 04 bottom right.
- Print the 30-piece batch **twice for 60 reversible pieces**. Use the
  single-piece file for extras.

Some source filenames use `othello`; these are the same hex board and
BioBuzz reversible pieces shown here.

### Colors and starting markers

Board slots:
1. **Black** — frame, walls, structural base, starting markers, and corner rings.
2. **Yellow** — pocket floors, joint tops, and underside ARES 23247 artwork.

Piece slots:
1. **Black** side and inlays.
2. **Yellow** side and inlays.

Filled black circles mark the three yellow-side starting pieces; hollow
black circles mark the three black-side starting pieces. The center cell
stays empty in the illustrated six-piece opening. Markers and corner rings
are flush with the pocket floors.

The 3MF files include surface colors, part names, and filament-slot assignments.
Load each file as one aligned multipart assembly. If your slicer keeps its
existing swatches, set them to the listed colors. Keep your own printer and
calibrated filament profiles.

### Printing and assembly

Print the board flat, pockets upward, at **100% scale**, **0.20 mm layer
height**, and **0.20 mm first layer**. Supports are not needed underneath
the board pockets or joints.

Assembled size: **289.9 × 322.7 × 5.2 mm**. The largest section is
179.4 × 168.6 mm. Supplied board placements fit a **270 × 270 mm bed**,
leaving at least 5 mm at the edges and space for a **40 × 40 mm purge tower**
at X=225–265, Y=225–265. Recheck clearance after arranging parts, enlarging
the tower, or adding a brim. Check the separate piece batch in your slicer too.

Revision v3_looser_joints has **0.40 mm nominal clearance around each dovetail**
and a **0.30 mm total gap between section edges** to reduce binding. Tile pockets
are unchanged. Lower sections vertically
into their matching sockets on a flat table. Move the board as separate sections.
The wider spacing of this sturdy revision does not align with earlier sections.

Geometry and color metadata have been checked. Print the small fit coupon
and inspect your sliced preview before producing a complete set. Both coupon
halves should sit flat separately and remain flat when joined without forcing.
Images are geometry renders, not photographs of completed prints.
