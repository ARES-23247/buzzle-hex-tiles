# BUZZLE — Hex Word Game | Yellow-Rim Board + Individual Letter Tiles

Give your word games a new shape. BUZZLE brings letter tiles to a 217-cell honeycomb board with bright yellow rims, colorful bonus spaces and room for satisfying crossings. Print a complete set or just the replacement letters you need.

## Play and explore

[Play BUZZLE online](https://aresfirst.org/buzzle) — local play for two to four people, computer opponents and two-player online games.

[BUZZLE Word Tools](https://aresfirst.org/buzzle/word-tools) — word checker, dictionary and two-letter list for tabletop play. No game or sign-in required. Checker/list work offline after setup; new definitions need internet.

## Printable rules and word reference

Keep the essentials beside your board: download **BUZZLE_Rules.pdf** for the
tabletop rules and **BUZZLE_Two_Letter_Words.pdf** for a one-page reference
containing 128 two-letter entries from BUZZLE's own word list.

For the current word checker, dictionary and two-letter list, visit
[BUZZLE Word Tools](https://aresfirst.org/buzzle/word-tools).

Build a large honeycomb word-game board with matching BioBuzz tiles. The
217-cell layout has letter and word multipliers, a central starting star,
and a seven-section board that assembles with dovetail joints.

The current sturdy revision uses 2.2 mm dividers, a 2.8 mm floor, and 3 mm
deep pockets. It fits the included **1.3-inch / 33.02 mm across-flats tiles**
at 100% scale. Tile thickness is 4.8 mm; pockets are 33.80 mm across flats.

### What to print

- **Board:** print Plate_01 through Plate_07 once each. Plate 01 is the
  37-cell center hub; each outer section has 30 cells.
- **Fit check:** print PRINT_FIRST_Pocket_and_Joint_Test before the full board.
- **100-letter set:** print each of the four BioBuzz Scrabble plates once.
- **Optional 144-letter set:** the six Hive-Swarm plates are an alternative
  set, not additional required plates for the 100-letter set.
- **Optional extras:** a 26-tile numbers/math plate, a BioBuzz token,
  plus individual A–Z and blank replacement tiles are included.

### Colors

The word tiles have **yellow bodies with black lettering and honeycomb artwork**.

Board filament slots:
1. Black — foundation, plain pocket floors, text, and rib bodies.
2. White — DW/KEY/start backgrounds.
3. Blue — DL and TL backgrounds.
4. Yellow — grid caps and TW backgrounds.

The 3MFs contain separate aligned parts, surface colors, and explicit filament
assignments. Import as a multipart assembly; part names begin with their slot
and color. If your slicer keeps its existing color swatches, set them to the
listed colors. Keep your own printer and calibrated filament profiles.

### Printing and assembly

Print the board flat with pockets facing upward at **0.20 mm layer height**
and a **0.20 mm first layer**. Board sections use flat geometry without support
under the pockets or joints. Keep everything at **100% scale**.

Assembled size: **541.8 × 613.4 × 5.8 mm**. The largest board section is
248.5 × 248.5 mm. The supplied board placements fit a 270 × 270 mm bed with
5 mm edge margins and room for a 40 × 40 mm purge tower at
X=225–265, Y=225–265. Check clearance again if you change placement, tower
size, or add a brim. Tile batch files retain their existing `plate_256`
layouts; check their tower placement in your slicer.

Test an actual tile and both dovetail halves using the coupon. The dovetails
have 0.40 mm nominal clearance, with a 0.30 mm total gap between section edges
(same tolerances as Buzzello). Assemble on a table, hub first, then lower
the outer sections into the sockets. Move the board as individual sections.

This revision has wider cell spacing than earlier versions, so print a
complete matching set. The geometry and color metadata have been checked;
the revised files still need your slicer preview and physical fit check.
