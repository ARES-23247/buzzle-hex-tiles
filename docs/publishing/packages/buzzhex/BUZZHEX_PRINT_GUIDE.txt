# BUZZHEX — 11 × 11 Hex with Buzzello tiles

**121 cells · six sections · existing 33.02 mm / 1.3-inch reversible tiles.**

![Board and print-bed preview](images/board_and_bed_preview.png)

## Print order

1. Print [the pocket-and-joint test](plates/PRINT_FIRST_Pocket_and_Joint_Test.3mf)
   at **100% scale**. Check one existing Buzzello tile in both pockets and slide
   the two test pieces together on a flat surface.
2. Print each of the six numbered plates below once, flat side down, at 100%.
   Each fits a 270 × 270 mm bed with a 5 mm margin and space for a 40 × 40 mm
   purge tower at X=225–265, Y=225–265. Preserve the supplied bed orientation.
3. Assemble on a flat table using the section positions in the image. The
   seams follow cell borders. Slide the tabs into their matching sockets;
   the tabs sit below the pocket floors. Do not mix with Buzzello board sections.
4. Use **121 reversible Buzzello tiles in a common supply**. Turn each tile to
   your color before placing it. A tile's large background identifies its color.

## Tiles to reuse or print

The existing tiles are unchanged: **33.02 mm across flats, 4.8 mm thick**,
black on one side and yellow on the other, with the original BioBuzz rosette.

- [Existing single tile](../../tiles/othello/3mf/hex_othello_piece_single.3mf)
- [Existing 30-tile plate](../../tiles/othello/plates/plate_30_pieces_print_twice.3mf)
- [Tile preview and print guide](../../tiles/othello/PRINT_GUIDE.md)

From scratch, print the 30-tile plate **four times**, plus **one single** (121).
If you already have 60 tiles, print **two more 30-tile plates and one single**
(61 additional). The retained filename says “print twice” because that was the
Buzzello quantity; the quantities above apply to BUZZHEX. Games often finish
before all 121 spaces fill, but this inventory covers any legal game.

## Filaments and dimensions

| Slot | Color | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Black | Foundation, walls, grid caps, and the A/K goal rails |
| 2 | Yellow | The 1/11 goal rails |
| 3 | White | Pocket floors and joint tops |

Use three filaments for the board; the shared pieces use black and yellow.
The white pocket floors help both tile colors stand out. These are geometry
previews, not photographs. Cell coordinates and section labels appear only
in the assembly diagram, not on the printed board.

- Pockets: **33.50 mm**, giving 0.24 mm clearance per flat face.
- Revision **v2_buzzhex_white_board** changes colors only; board geometry and fit are unchanged.
- Cell-center pitch: **35.70 mm**; dividers: **2.20 mm**.
- Solid floor: **2.80 mm**; pocket depth: **2.40 mm**; total: **5.20 mm**.
- Tile projection above the walls: **2.40 mm**.
- Socket clearance: **0.40 mm**; assembled seam gap: **0.30 mm**.
- Goal rails extend the outer cell boundary by **6 mm**.
- Use 0.20 mm layers; colored rail caps occupy the top two layers.
- Assembled board: approximately **671.4 × 404.7 mm** (26.4 × 15.9 inches).
- See [the measured manifest](print_manifest.json) for the assembled size and
  each plate's exact placement, and [color import help](../../../docs/3MF_COLOR_IMPORT.md).

Meshes, material partitions, pocket dimensions, seams, and bed placement pass
automated checks. Physical fit has not yet been tested on a printer; print
the small test before committing to the six board plates.
