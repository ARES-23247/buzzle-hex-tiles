# BUZZELLO — Classic & Large Hex Strategy Boards

Surround, flip, and claim the hive. BUZZELLO brings black-and-yellow strategy to
the table with chunky reversible pieces and a honeycomb board that keeps every
move in place. Choose the familiar **Classic 61-cell board** or spread out onto
the **Large 91-cell board**, which adds a complete outer ring.

Both editions use the same **1.3-inch / 33.02 mm pieces**. Keep your existing
pieces and print a matching set of four board sections in your chosen size.

[Play BUZZELLO online](https://aresfirst.org/buzzello) with a friend on one device,
invite someone to a private match, or challenge the computer.

## Included files

Each edition has four board sections, a small pocket-and-joint fit test, preview
images, and a print guide. The ZIPs include both **3MF and STL** options, plus
the shared single-piece and 30-piece batch 3MF files.

- **3MF:** includes aligned parts, Black/Yellow color names, and filament assignments.
- **Single-color STL:** merged solid geometry for each section and the fit test.
- **Color-part STLs:** aligned Black and Yellow components. Import each matching
  pair as one assembly and assign the filaments yourself; STL cannot store colors.

Print each of the four numbered sections once, from the same edition. If you
already have 60 playing pieces, add one for Classic or 31 for Large.

## Built for a sturdy, flat assembly

Both boards have **2.2 mm dividers**, **2.8 mm floors**, and **2.4 mm deep pockets**,
for a total height of **5.2 mm**. The pieces remain easy to lift and flip.
Both retain **0.40 mm nominal dovetail clearance** and a **0.30 mm total seam gap**
to reduce the binding reported with earlier prints. These dimensions have been
checked digitally; the revised fit still needs a physical print test.

Print the fit coupon first. Both halves should rest flat individually and stay
flat when joined without force. Print the board pockets upward, at 100% scale,
with 0.20 mm layers and a 0.20 mm first layer. No supports are required beneath
the board pockets or joints.

Each supplied board section fits a **270 × 270 mm bed**, with at least 5 mm edge
clearance and room for a 40 × 40 mm purge tower at X=225–265, Y=225–265. Recheck
the sliced layout if you rearrange parts or add a brim. Check the piece batch
separately in your slicer.

Board colors are **Black** for the structural frame, walls, and starting markers,
and **Yellow** for the pocket floors and joint tops. Pieces are black and yellow
on opposite faces. Use your calibrated printer and filament profiles and verify
the sliced color preview. Gallery images are geometry renders, not print photos.
