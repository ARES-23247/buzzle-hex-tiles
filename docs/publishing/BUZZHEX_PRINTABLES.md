# BUZZHEX — 11 x 11 Hex Strategy Game | Reuse Your BUZZELLO Pieces

One tile. Six directions. A race to connect the hive.

BUZZHEX turns classic Hex into a tactile tabletop game with chunky reversible
pieces, recessed pockets and bold goal rails. Link your two matching edges before
your opponent does. The rules take minutes to learn, and every move changes the board.

Created by **ARES FTC 23247** for game nights and robotics outreach. Already printed
BUZZELLO? Keep your **1.3-inch / 33.02 mm pieces** and give them a new game to play.

[Play BUZZHEX on our website](https://aresfirst.org/buzzhex) — two players sharing
one device, with the same 11 x 11 board and opening swap rule.

## Your printable set

- **121-cell board:** six interlocking sections for a 270 x 270 mm bed.
- **Print-first fit test:** check a tile and the joints before printing the full set.
- **Shared pieces:** single-piece and 30-piece 3MF files, with black/yellow faces.
- **Rules PDF:** a one-page tabletop reference including the opening swap rule.
- **Print and color guides:** assembly order, filament assignments and quantities.

Print each numbered board section once at 100% scale. From scratch, print the
30-piece plate **four times plus one single** for a full 121-piece supply. If you
already have 60 pieces, add two 30-piece batches and one single. The legacy piece
filenames contain “othello” and “print twice”; the BUZZHEX quantities above apply.

## Three board colors, two piece colors

**Slot 1: Black** — frame, dividers and A/K goal rails.  
**Slot 2: Yellow** — 1/11 goal rails.  
**Slot 3: White** — pocket floors and joint tops.

White pockets keep both playing colors easy to see. The reversible pieces use
black and yellow only. Keep each 3MF as one aligned multipart object, retain the
named parts and assign these colors if your slicer keeps its own filament swatches.
Use your calibrated printer and filament profiles; check the sliced color preview.

Gallery images show the intended colors and are geometry renders, not print photos.
Printables' interactive viewer may show generic orange or hide flush details.

## Built for your print bed

The assembled board measures approximately **671.4 x 404.7 x 5.2 mm**. Each supplied
section fits a **270 x 270 mm bed**, with a 5 mm margin and room for a 40 x 40 mm
purge tower at X=225–265, Y=225–265. Preserve the supplied plate orientation and
recheck clearance if adding a brim or rearranging parts. Check piece batches separately.

The board has **2.2 mm dividers, 2.8 mm floors and 2.4 mm deep pockets**. The 33.50 mm
pockets fit existing 33.02 mm pieces. Joints have 0.40 mm nominal socket clearance
and a 0.30 mm total seam gap to reduce binding. Digital checks pass; physical fit
still needs a print test. Print the small coupon first and check it on a flat surface.

Print flat at 100% scale with 0.20 mm layers. Assemble all six sections using the
diagram; do not mix BUZZHEX and BUZZELLO board sections.

## How to play

Start with an empty board. Black connects the black A/K edges; Yellow connects the
yellow 1/11 edges. Take turns placing one tile, your color face up, in an empty
cell. A continuous chain through shared hex edges wins immediately. Tiles stay
where they are placed: no flipping or capturing.

After the first Black tile, the second player may swap colors instead of placing
a tile. The opening tile stays Black and stays in its original cell; the first
player becomes Yellow and moves next. This one-time swap balances the opening.
Download the included rules PDF for the complete setup and examples.
