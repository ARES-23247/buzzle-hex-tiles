## 🎮 Game 1: "HEX-WORDS" (Free-form Tabletop Word Battle)

* **Players**: 2–4 players
* **Time**: 30–45 minutes
* **Components**: Letter tiles (A–Z with score subscripts + Blanks)
* **Board**: None needed (played open on any table)

### 📖 How to Play
1. **Setup**: Place all letter tiles face-down in the central "Bag/Pool". Each player draws **7 tiles** into their hand.
2. **First Word**: Player 1 plays a valid word of 2+ letters anywhere in the center of the table.
3. **The 3 Hex Axes**: Hexagonal tiles touch along 6 flat edges, forming 3 continuous lines of sight ($0^\circ$ horizontal, $60^\circ$ diagonal-right, $120^\circ$ diagonal-left). Words can be placed in any of these 3 directions!
4. **Turn Play**: On your turn, add 1 or more tiles from your hand to build upon existing tiles. Every newly created contiguous line of adjacent tiles must form a valid dictionary word.
5. **Special Hex Scoring**:
   * **Base Points**: Sum of letter score subscripts on all newly formed or extended words.
   * 🍯 **The Honeycomb Bonus (+15 pts)**: If your placed tiles complete a closed 6-tile hexagonal ring surrounding an empty space (a "Honeycomb Cell"), you immediately score a **+15 point Honeycomb Bonus**!
   * 🌟 **Tri-Intersection Bonus (2x Total Turn Score)**: If a single placed tile simultaneously completes valid words along **all 3 axes at once**, double your entire turn's score!
6. **Ending**: The game ends when the tile pool is empty and one player uses their last tile. Highest total score wins.

## ⚡ Game 2: "HEXAGRAMS" (High-Speed Word Race)

* **Players**: 2–6 players (or Solo Time Attack)
* **Time**: 10–15 minutes
* **Components**: Complete Letter Pool

### 📖 How to Play
1. **Setup**: Place all letter tiles face-down in the center "Bunch". Each player draws **12 tiles**.
2. **The Race**: Any player shouts **"SWARM!"**. All players simultaneously flip their tiles face-up and race to arrange all their letters into their own connected hexagonal word web.
3. **The "PEEL!" Call**: When a player has successfully incorporated all of their tiles into valid connected words, they shout **"PEEL!"**. Every player (including the caller) must immediately take **1 new tile** from the Bunch and incorporate it into their grid.
4. **The "DUMP!" Rule**: If a player is stuck with unwanted letters (e.g. Q, Z, X), they may shout **"DUMP!"**, return 1 tile to the Bunch, and draw **2 new random tiles**.
5. **Winning**: When there are fewer tiles remaining in the Bunch than players, the first player to assemble all their tiles into valid words shouts **"HIVE-LOCK!"** and wins the round!

## 🔢 Game 3: "NUMBER HIVE" / "EQUATION CLASH"

* **Players**: 2–4 players
* **Time**: 20–30 minutes
* **Components**: Number tiles (0–9) and Math Operator tiles ($+, -, \times, \div, =$)

### 📖 How to Play
1. **Setup**: Each player draws **6 Number tiles** and **2 Operator tiles**.
2. **Playing Equations**: Players place tiles along any straight hex line to form true mathematical statements.
   * Example: `[3]` `[+]` `[5]` `[=]` `[8]`
   * Example: `[9]` `[x]` `[4]` `[=]` `[3]` `[6]`
3. **Branching Math**: You can branch off any existing number on the board. For example, using the `[8]` from the first equation, another player can branch across another axis to play `[8]` `[x]` `[2]` `[=]` `[1]` `[6]`.
4. **Scoring**:
   * Score = The numerical value of the equation result (e.g. `$36$` points for `$9 \times 4 = 36$`).
   * **Multiplication / Division Bonus**: $+5$ extra points for equations containing `[x]` or `[/]`.
   * **Compound Equation Bonus**: $+10$ points for chaining operations (e.g. `[2]` `[+]` `[3]` `[x]` `[4]` `[=]` `[1]` `[4]`).

## 🏆 Game 4: "HONEYCOMB CROSSWORD" (Board Game)

* **Players**: 2–4 players
* **Board**: Printable **[`hex_game_board_1.5in.pdf`](file:///C:/Users/david/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/hex-scrabble-tiles/printouts/hex_game_board_1.5in.pdf)**

### 📖 How to Play
1. Players draw 7 tiles and start from the central **Starting Star (★)**.
2. Players score premium bonuses when placing tiles onto colored multiplier hexes:
   * **2x Letter (2L - Light Blue)**: Doubles the point value of that letter.
   * **3x Letter (3L - Deep Blue)**: Triples the point value of that letter.
   * **2x Word (2W - Light Coral)**: Doubles the entire word's point value.
   * **3x Word (3W - Deep Red)**: Triples the entire word's point value.
3. If a word covers multiple word multipliers, they multiply together (e.g., covering two 2W hexes yields a **4x word score**)!

## 🧠 Game 5: "TARGET 24 & ROSETTE MATH" (Solo / Co-op Puzzle)

* **Players**: 1–2 players
* **Components**: Number & Operator tiles + **[`number_hive_math_puzzles.pdf`](file:///C:/Users/david/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/hex-scrabble-tiles/printouts/number_hive_math_puzzles.pdf)**

### 📖 How to Play
1. Place a target number tile (e.g. `[2]` `[4]`) into the yellow center hex of a 7-hex Rosette.
2. Using the given 6 perimeter numbers and operator tiles, find an arrangement where reading adjacent 3-tile or 5-tile chains around the ring evaluates to the target number.
3. Great for daily brainteasers, speed math duels, and family game nights!
