🎮 5 Games You Can Play Right Now

1. HEX-WORDS (Free-form Tabletop Crossword)
2–4 Players30–45 MinStrategy

Play crossword words across 3 continuous hexagonal axes on any open table. Features the Honeycomb Ring Bonus (+15 pts) when a player completes a 6-tile closed loop around an empty space, and Tri-Intersection Double Score when completing words along 3 axes simultaneously!

2. HEXAGRAMS (High-Speed Simultaneous Word Race)
2–6 Players / Solo10–15 MinFast Paced

A frantic, no-turns speed race! Everyone starts with 12 tiles and races to connect them all into a valid hexagonal word cluster. Call 'PEEL!' when your tiles are used to force everyone to draw more, or 'DUMP!' to trade in bad letters.

3. NUMBER HIVE / EQUATION CLASH
2–4 Players20–30 MinMath & Logic

Build branching numerical equations across the 3 hex axes (e.g. [3] [+] [5] [=] [8] crossing with [8] [x] [2] [=] [1] [6]). Score points equal to the calculated results with multipliers for multiplication and division.

4. HONEYCOMB CROSSWORD (Board Game)
2–4 Players30–60 MinClassic Board

Played on the printable 1.5" calibrated game board. Start on the center Star (★) and expand outwards to control Double Letter, Triple Letter, Double Word, and Triple Word bonus tiles.

5. TARGET 24 & ROSETTE MATH (Solo / Co-op Puzzle)
1–2 Players5–10 Min per PuzzleBrain Teaser

Arrange 6 perimeter numbers and operators around a central target hex to evaluate to the target number. Great for daily math puzzles, kids, and puzzle enthusiasts.
